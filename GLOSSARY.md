# MothRef

## Language

**Board**:
An artist's reference canvas containing images and other reference material.

**Image burst**:
Images added to a board together by a paste or file drop, with their arrangement belonging to the same undo operation.

**Library**:
The collection of known boards and the albums used to organize them.

**Library home**:
The main library screen, showing albums and boards that do not belong to an album. Opening an album shows its contents.
_Avoid_: General gallery.

**Album**:
A named group of boards, presented in the library as a stack of previews. A board belongs to at most one album; moving it to another album changes its place without making a copy. Removing an album preserves its boards.
_Avoid_: Folder (for this library grouping; folders on disk are separate).
