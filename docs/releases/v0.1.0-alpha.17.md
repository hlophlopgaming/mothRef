This preview adds groups, drawing sessions, multiple board windows and a refreshed interface.

### Changes

- The zoom field in the bottom toolbar shows whole percents at a fixed width, so the toolbar no longer changes width while zooming.
- Fix scrolling the Layers drawer (or any panel over the board) zooming the board underneath.
- The grayscale view can be toned with a color: Settings › Appearance › Grayscale view tint (for example sepia or blue). Shadows stay dark and highlights light; the middle tones take the color.
- Fix locking or switching to grayscale on one window's board affecting another window until its board changed.
- Fix boards with groups opening as a partial "Untitled" board from the library.
- Fix a group backdrop that sometimes stayed too large after an image was dragged out of it.
- Fix the View window bar popping in place instead of sliding down from the top edge; it now slides in and out quickly.
- Fix the navigator ignoring its opacity since it shows thumbnails. Settings › Canvas › "Images in the navigator" switches between thumbnails and plain blocks.
- A calmer interface: the title bar is a quiet strip where the open board sits on a soft tab and other boards are plain text, with a dot for unsaved changes; icon buttons have no box until you point at them; the Layers drawer is a compact tree with fold arrows, group colors and type icons; selection bars show plain words; the arrange question after adding images is a small dialog with four picture choices, and Esc leaves the images as they are; the shortcut hint is a short-lived pill without buttons.
- The minimap shows real thumbnails of the board with the visible area framed. A custom selection color stays the accent while selected rows and buttons get a calm tone of it. Context menus list only what applies to the click, in compact rows. The added-images toast and the library follow the design: a recovery card in the corner instead of a blocking dialog, a search field with an icon, and counts like "1 board" in plain text.
- Select all (Ctrl/⌘+A) selects everything on the board, or inside the group you entered.
- Drawing sessions (gesture mode, F5 or View ▸ Drawing session): practice with timed images from the whole board, the selection or a group. Pick one time per image (30 s to 10 min) or a plan of stages such as 10 × 30 s then 5 × 2 min, and save plans for later. Images are shuffled each once, shown in board order or picked at random. A 3-2-1 countdown, a ring timer that turns pink in the last 5 seconds, a soft chime on every change, pause (Space), previous and next (arrow keys) and "+30 s" are there; the panel hides while you draw. Run it full screen or in a small window kept above your drawing app that takes the shape of each image. The summary lists what was shown and for how long; star images and repeat the same order, a new shuffle, only skipped or only starred images.
- Fix "Resize window to selection" leaving the board drawn at the old size on Wayland.
- Drawing sessions: type any time per image or per stage (minutes and seconds) besides the presets; saved plans get a name, and a right click deletes one.
- Group titles and colors show at once instead of after the change reaches the disk, and the title field opens immediately after Ctrl/⌘+G.
- Fix the window staying at the mini session size after a session, and the Layers drawer's close button doing nothing without a selection.
- Groups: select images and notes and press Ctrl/⌘+G to put them on a tinted backdrop that grows and shrinks with its content, then type a title (Enter keeps it, Esc leaves it untitled). Click a member or the title to select the whole group and drag to move it; double-click to step inside and work on single items. Drop an item onto a backdrop to add it, drag it out to take it out. Groups nest, have six backdrop colors, and Ctrl/⌘+Shift+G dissolves them. Images pasted or dropped onto a backdrop join that group. A Layers drawer on the right edge shows the board as a tree; drag a row onto a group to move it in, onto an item to place it above that item, or onto the "Layers" header to take it out to the board. Exports draw backdrops and group titles. Boards with groups are saved as `.moth` schema 6; older MothRef versions cannot open them.
- Glass background blurs what is behind the window on Windows 11 (acrylic) and KDE Plasma (Wayland and X11), in addition to macOS. Other systems keep the translucent tint.

- Move board tabs between windows: drag a tab out of the tab strip to open it in its own window, or use the tab menu (right click or long press) to move it to a new or another window, or to gather all tabs into one window. On Windows, macOS and X11 a tab can also be dropped onto another window's tab strip. Each window has its own tabs, view mode, pin and video memory share.
- Tabs: a "+" button for a new board (Ctrl/⌘+N), middle-click closes a tab, and boards never saved to a file are shown in italics.
- Over shows a one-time hint on how to leave it; Settings › Window and overlay can show it again.
- Pin the board above a chosen window on GNOME Wayland (with the MothRef window extension, which Settings offers to update) and on macOS, where the board follows the chosen application.

### Validation

- Local formatting, workspace check and workspace Clippy with warnings denied passed.
- All 369 workspace tests passed locally, with no failures or ignored tests, including group persistence/truncation, undo/redo, rendering golden images and allocation checks. The sandboxed run lacked socket/data-directory permissions; the unrestricted repeat passed.
- All 30 corpus fixtures passed; 50 process kills lost no acknowledged commands.
- `cargo deny check` passed advisories, bans, licenses and sources.
- The sequential quick benchmark passed: 10,000 distinct images on llvmpipe, p99 25.359 ms and 13.32 MiB textures; 4K JPEG tiles measured 12.603 ms without cache and 0.029 ms with memory cache. These software-renderer measurements do not qualify native hardware targets.
- The `.moth` reader passed a 90-second fuzz run (2,508,923 inputs) outside the sandbox, including LeakSanitizer completion. The first sandboxed run reached its time limit without a reader failure but could not complete LeakSanitizer because ptrace was restricted.
- Publication requires passing CI for the release commit and all four binary builds.

### Compatibility and limitations

Boards now save as schema 6, including groups; schemas 1–6 remain readable. Older MothRef versions cannot open schema-6 boards.

Windows/macOS native launch and window transitions, HiDPI, device loss with several windows and real-hardware glass blur remain incompletely qualified; see docs/M3-WINDOW-VALIDATION.md. Binaries are unsigned and macOS builds are not notarized.

Tier-3 reliability checks remain CI/maintainer-only. The five-minute soak is reserved for release 1.0.
